docs: what the deadline drill established, and what it did not

A real cohort through a real deadline on a Team organization, with three test
accounts on an org that has no students. OPEN-ITEMS 3 now records what was
measured rather than argued - the student who is admin of their own repository
getting 404 from every angle at the organization ruleset, the late push
excluded while the live repository keeps it, the reopen that takes one id out
and leaves the cohort locked, a second finalize pass that does not undo it, the
migration and its inverse, and the free-plan degradation with GitHub's own two
403s naming the two plan gates.

It stays OPEN, and for two reasons worth stating rather than glossing: the
reopen was driven through unlockability + applyUnlock with the arguments
AssignmentDetailView passes, not by clicking the button, so the SPA's own wiring
is still only covered by tests/e2e; and three accounts the operator controls on
an organization with nothing at stake is not a cohort. The closing condition is
reworded to say so.
